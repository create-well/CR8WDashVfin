# THS-CANONICAL-SOP-GOV-RACI

## Purpose
Establish the required governance, RACI, decision-gate, escalation, evidence, and approval structure for every Take Home Studio SOP.

## Decision gates

| Gate | Decision | Accountable | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Opportunity / partnership | Walbert | Monica |
| D02 | Proposal / pricing / terms | Monica | Jerjon |
| D03 | Scope / schedule / delivery | Christine | Monica |
| D04 | Creative direction / final delivery | Ayce | Walbert |
| D05 | Research method / integrity / publication | Sarah | Andrew |
| D06 | Contractor / budget / payment release | Monica | Christine |

## Control
No SOP becomes Active until exactly one accountable role is confirmed, an owner and approver are named, governance dependencies are linked, and the assumptions register is reviewed.

## Escalation
Acknowledge within 24 hours, assign within 48 hours, and escalate unresolved governance or legal blockers within 72 hours.
