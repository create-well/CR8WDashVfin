Create Well

CR8W Dashboard — the private home base of the Create Well co-creation collective. The dashboard gathers the collective's week in one place — moves, flows, content, money, and decisions — mirrored read-only from the team's Notion workspace. Members sign in with their own account and can optionally connect Google Calendar to view and sync collective events; calendar data is used only inside this dashboard, never sold or shared.
